camloc · Yas MarinaThe ideaSheet 3 / 6
The guiding analogy

A rally co-driver with a pace-note book.

The co-driver…
…is this block in camloc (causal, every frame)
Counts distance and turns from the car's own speed and steering between landmarks. Smooth, but it drifts.
Odometry + relative yawWheel speeds + steering with a braking-aware axle choice (or validation/twist); keyframe rotation from LK flow + essential matrix.
Recognises a landmark from the pace notes and snaps back to the map. Absolute, but only now and then.
Geo-referenced map + PnPCLAHE ORB features matched to a 3D map built from training laps; PnP + RANSAC per camera gives an absolute fix in metres.
Judges each sighting: do two eyes agree, and does it fit where we should be? Look-alikes are rejected.
Consensus + NIS gate + EKFCross-camera agreement, a statistical consistency test that can recover, and an EKF (x, y, yaw, sideslip) on track and pit geometry.
Dropped mid-stage with no position: looks around until sightings agree, commits provisionally, and changes their mind if the evidence says so.
No start poseGlobal search over the map → provisional lock (2 cameras or 3 frames agree) → confirm after 2 accepted fixes, or switch when ≥ 3 rejected fixes agree elsewhere.
Declarations: no pre-trained models, no external data; OpenCV ORB / LK / PnP only. Car signals: wheel speeds, steering, brake (allowed set, ADR-0003). The GT pose and /tf map→base_link are never read at inference.

Why each choice · and the evidence

Every block earns its place with a measurement.

ChoiceWhyEvidence
Classical, not deep learning or VLMsOne night of data, so nothing to overfit; explainable; deterministic; real-time on a CPU24–39 ms per frame on an M4, against a 100 ms frame budget
Geo-referenced map + PnPKnown 3D geometry gives absolute metres, not just relative motionMap fixes take the error from 107 m (odometry alone) to under 1 m
Odometry + EKFSmooth, causal, and it fills the gaps between fixes30 s without any map fix: 2.7 m, and it recovers when fixes return
Gating + consensusA wrong landmark is worse than none: reject outliers before they reach the filter2 % of fixes injected 10 m wrong: lap 4 0.457 m vs 0.460 m clean
Provisional lockCommit early so the output is never silent, but never trust a single sighting16 / 16 mid-lap starts end correct; a 13.6 m alias switches in 0.3 s
Strict validationThe reason these numbers can be trusted: map and every calibration rebuilt without the scored lap; the official scorer; causal replaySame 0.513 m with or without a GT start pose; calibration leakage measured at ~0.02 m
