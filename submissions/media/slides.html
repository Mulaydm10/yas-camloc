camloc · Yas MarinaPipelineSheet 3 / 6
Pipeline · causal, every frame

A known map, odometry and one filter. No neural networks.

InputCausal MCAP replay: images grouped by stamp, never ahead of time
PredictWheel speeds + steering, braking-aware axle choice (or validation/twist)
Relative yawKeyframe rotation: LK flow + essential matrix
Absolute fixCLAHE ORB features → geo-referenced 3D map → PnP + RANSAC per camera, weighted by inliers and reprojection error
GateCross-camera consensus, then a NIS test that recovers if the filter itself is wrong
EKFx, y, yaw, keyframe yaw, online sideslip · track and pit-lane geometry
No start pose

Find yourself, then check.

Global search: descriptor votes over the whole map → PnP on the top 8
Provisional lock: 2 cameras agree, or 3 frames agree after odometry
Confirm after 2 accepted fixes, or switch when ≥ 3 rejected fixes agree elsewhere
Time-boxed search; never silent after 3 s
Declarations: no pre-trained models, no external data. OpenCV ORB / LK / PnP only. Car signals used: wheel speeds, steering, brake pressure (allowed set, ADR-0003). The GT pose and /tf map→base_link are never read at inference; GT is used only for training-time calibration, the map and scoring.
